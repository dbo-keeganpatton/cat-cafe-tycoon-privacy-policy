# Privacy Policy for Cat Cafe Tycoon

**Effective date:** 2026-07-30
**App:** Cat Cafe Tycoon (package `com.keegan.catcafe`)
**Publisher:** Keegan Patton
**Contact:** keeganpatton@gmail.com

This policy explains what data Cat Cafe Tycoon and its third-party
partners collect, how it is used, and your choices. It applies to the Android
app distributed via Google Play.

## Summary

- The app itself **does not collect any personal information**, does not
  require an account, and does not sync your progress to any server.
- Your game progress is stored **only on your device** as a local save file.
- The app displays ads via **Google AdMob**, which collects an anonymous
  device Advertising ID to serve and measure ads.

## Data collected by the app

**None.** Cat Cafe Tycoon does not collect, transmit, or store any personal
information about you. There is no login, no user account, no analytics SDK,
and no cloud save.

Your gameplay progress (coins, generators owned, prestige currency,
preferences) is stored in a local save file (`save.json`) inside the app's
private storage on your device. It is not readable by other apps and it is
not sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## Data collected by third parties

The app displays ads through **Google AdMob**, a Google Ireland Limited /
Google LLC service. When ads are requested and displayed, AdMob may collect:

- Your device's **Advertising ID** (a resettable, anonymous identifier
  Android provides for advertising)
- **Device information**: model, operating system version, language,
  approximate coarse location derived from IP address
- **Ad interaction data**: which ads were shown, clicked, and completed
  (e.g. finished watching a rewarded video)

This data is used by Google to serve ads, measure ad performance, prevent
fraud, and — where you have consented — personalize ads. Google's use of
data is governed by its own privacy policy:

- Google Privacy Policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- How Google uses information from partner sites and apps:
  <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>
- AdMob & AdSense data disclosures:
  <https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543>

Cat Cafe Tycoon does not use any other third-party SDKs for analytics,
crash reporting, attribution, or advertising.

## Children

Cat Cafe Tycoon is rated for a **general audience aged 13 and up** and is
not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal
information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has provided
information through the app, please contact keeganpatton@gmail.com and any
associated data will be handled according to applicable law.

## Your choices

- **Reset or opt out of the Advertising ID**: on Android, go to
  Settings → Privacy → Ads. You can reset your Advertising ID or delete it
  entirely to opt out of personalized advertising.
- **Uninstall the app**: this removes the local save file and stops all ad
  requests from this app on your device.
- **Ad-related choices with Google**:
  <https://adssettings.google.com>

## Data retention

The app stores your local save file until you uninstall the app or clear
its data. The app does not retain data anywhere else. Third-party ad
partners retain data according to their own policies (see the Google links
above).

## Security

Data exchanged with Google's ad servers is transmitted over HTTPS. The
local save file is stored in the app's private sandbox on your device,
which the Android operating system isolates from other apps.

## International users

The app is distributed globally through Google Play. Ad requests made by
AdMob may transfer data to and be processed in the United States or other
countries where Google operates its infrastructure. By using the app, you
consent to this processing.

## California residents (CCPA)

Cat Cafe Tycoon does not sell or share personal information for cross-
context behavioral advertising in the meaning of the California Consumer
Privacy Act. Advertising-related data collection is handled by Google
AdMob under its own privacy commitments; see Google's California disclosures
in the links above.

## European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland (GDPR)

The legal basis for processing ad-related data is your consent, obtained
via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form shown on first
launch in applicable regions. You may withdraw consent at any time by
resetting your Advertising ID (see "Your choices" above).

You have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data held by
Google in connection with advertising; requests should be directed to
Google through the settings and links above, as the app publisher does not
receive or store this data.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the effective date at the top will be
updated and the new version will be published at the same URL.
